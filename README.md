I build small, useful things and publish how they work.

**[YardFigure](https://yardfigure.com)** — six free calculators for home and garden projects: soil,
mulch, gravel, concrete, sand and pavers. Enter three measurements, get the quantity in cubic yards,
bags or tons. No signup.

I am a developer, not a contractor. That is exactly why every calculator shows its full working, why
every depth figure names its source, and why
**[the formulas are public](https://github.com/S-A-Wasey/yardfigure-formulas)** — if you cannot check
my numbers, you have no reason to trust them.
